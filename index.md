# Corporate Finance Study Guide

A practical, rigorous study guide for Penn builders, Wharton quantitative finance students, and candidates preparing for investment banking and corporate finance interviews. It covers the core analytical toolkit: time value of money, risk and cost of capital, capital structure, project valuation, free cash flow modeling, enterprise valuation, multiples, and M&A dynamics — with clean math and zero fluff.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic in the navigation is a **standalone page readable in ~10 minutes**.
- Every page follows an identical, interview-tested shape: **why it matters**, **core concepts** with explicit formulas, an intuitive **mental model** (ASCII diagram), **interview questions** with detailed answer keys, and a curated **watch list** of verified lectures.
- Topics are sequenced logically from foundational cost-of-capital building blocks to enterprise valuation and deal mechanics. You can work through them linearly or jump directly to specific interview hotspots.
- Interview badges highlight prep priority: **🎯 Frequent** (core IB / corp fin technicals) and **Occasional** (specialized or situational topics).
- Nothing here requires an external service, database, or sign-up — just clean, scannable Markdown.
